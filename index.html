Privacy policy

Maintained by Bob Yang

A personal connection to Google Drive.

This OAuth application, named rclone, lets Bob Yang authorize the open-source rclone command-line tool to work with his own Google Drive.

What it does

rclone can list, copy, back up, and synchronize files between Google Drive, the operator's devices, and storage destinations the operator selects. Depending on the permissions granted and commands run, it can also create, move, update, or delete files.

This client is for the operator's personal use. It is not offered as a hosted file service or as a sign-in service for the public.

How access works

Google asks for permission during authorization. rclone then uses that authorization on the operator's devices to perform requested file operations. This informational website does not receive Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

The privacy policy explains data access, storage, sharing, retention, and how to revoke permission.

About this application

This is an independently configured personal OAuth client, not the official rclone project and not a Google product. For the software itself, visit rclone.org.
